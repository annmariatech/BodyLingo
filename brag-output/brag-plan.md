# Brag Plan: BodyLingo

## What is this app?
BodyLingo is a learning space for exploring communication cues with curiosity, context, and care.

## The angle
One cue can invite more than one interpretation. The video follows a learner from noticing a furrowed brow, through a short lesson and thoughtful quiz choice, into a branching practice moment. Treat the product with quiet confidence and let its careful language carry the emotion.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
“A furrowed brow.” The cue appears in a real lesson card, followed by “What might it mean?” as several gentle, noncommittal possibilities fan out.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The real lesson “Facial Expressions Without Assumptions” opens in the BodyLingo learning interface.
- The quiz asks what to do when someone is frowning; “Consider the context and ask gently…” is selected and receives the in-product “That’s right.” response.
- A branching scenario card invites the learner to “Try a moment from everyday life.”

## Outro / punchline
“There’s more than one way to connect.” Resolve to the BodyLingo mark and the site’s line: “communication, with care.”

## User flow worth showing
Open a lesson → notice a communication cue and answer a quiz → explore a possible response in branching practice.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet, human-centered product film
- Interpretation: slow, confident reveals with generous type and restrained transitions; the product’s real UI and careful copy stay in focus.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 22 seconds

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — One visible cue — 3s
A pale sage canvas. A real-looking BodyLingo lesson card arrives with “A furrowed brow or a brief smile”; the question “What might it mean?” opens space beside it. This cue and lesson phrasing come from the lesson content. A small penguin mark anchors the scene.
Sequential/interaction: yes — cue card settles first, then two quiet context cards appear: “The situation” and “Their own words.”
Audio intent: warm, understated start.
Audio-coupled idea: the second possibility card lands near the 1.635s strong beat; hold the question so it reads.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The lesson — 4s
Recreate the real learning view: BodyLingo sidebar, “Lessons for real life,” and the lesson “Facial Expressions Without Assumptions.” The teal-green canvas eases into the product surface.
Sequential/interaction: yes — lesson card opens as if selected.
Audio intent: calm product reveal.
Audio-coupled idea: one beat-aligned card reveal, with a full settled hold.
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — A thoughtful answer — 5s
Show the real quiz prompt: “You notice someone frowning during a conversation. What is a useful next step?” Two distractors remain visible but quiet; the correct option, “Consider the context and ask gently if something is on their mind,” receives a green selection state, followed by “That’s right.”
Sequential/interaction: yes — a cursor selects the contextual answer; response appears afterward.
Audio intent: one restrained confirmation.
Audio-coupled idea: selection click at the moment the option settles; keep the prompt and selected answer legible.
Transition mood: soft slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Practice with possibility — 6s
Show the app’s real scenario library headline “Try a moment from everyday life.” A scenario card comes forward with one choice path branching into two softly colored outcome cards, suggesting practice without implying one universal reading.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the scenario card is selected; two possible response paths appear one at a time.
Audio intent: thoughtful continuation.
Audio-coupled idea: the small branch dot lands near the 15.285s strong beat; the outcome copy has a long hold.
Transition mood: slow crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Brand close — 4s
The BodyLingo “b.” mark and wordmark resolve over the site’s pale sage background. Hold the exact line “There’s more than one way to connect.” followed by “communication, with care.”
Sequential/interaction: yes — headline, then wordmark/tagline; each gets a settled readable hold.
Audio intent: warm, quiet finish; music fades gently.
Audio-coupled idea: the accent rule lands near the 19.655s strong beat after the full lockup has settled.
Transition mood: soft fade to end
